Privacy Policy

This policy describes what data the NOVION FLOW Windows desktop application and its license server process, why, for how long, and how the data is protected. In short: the documents you process stay on your computer, except the signed documents you yourself send to banks from your own email account. The license server only knows what is needed for the license.

This is the English version of our Politika privatnosti.

Data controller

Marko Mijatović, individual — author of the NOVION FLOW application
Privacy contact: novionflow@gmail.com

What is never sent to our server

  • the content of PDF documents, file names and paths;
  • case numbers and names of parties from the documents;
  • the smart card PIN and the private key (the private key never leaves the card);
  • any data from your Google account, including access and refresh tokens, email content and recipients.

Sending documents to banks and access to your Gmail account

After signing, NOVION FLOW can send the signed documents to banks from an email account you connect. When you connect a Gmail account by signing in with Google, the application requests only these permissions:

  • your email address (openid, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email) — to show which account the mail is sent from and to verify that the same account is still connected;
  • send email on your behalf (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send) — to send the emails to banks that you have reviewed and confirmed.

The application does not read, search, delete or modify your mail and has no access to your contacts. It sends only the emails you have reviewed and explicitly confirmed in the application: one email per document and bank, with the signed PDF attached. You can see the sent messages in the "Sent" folder of your account.

How Google user data is used, stored and shared

  • Access: your email address and the permission to send email on your behalf, nothing else.
  • Use: the email address is shown in the application as the sender and used to check the connected account; the send permission is used only to send emails you have confirmed.
  • Storage: the OAuth tokens and your email address are stored only on your computer, encrypted. They are never stored on our server or anywhere else.
  • Sharing: Google user data is not transferred to our server or to any third party. The only recipients of an email are the bank addresses you confirmed.
  • Retention and deletion: the tokens stay on your computer until you disconnect the account. Choosing "Odjavi nalog" (Sign out) in the application deletes them from your computer. You can also revoke access at any time on your Google Account permissions page. A local sending log (without addresses, subjects or content) is kept on your computer only to prevent sending the same document twice.

NOVION FLOW's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, do not sell it, do not transfer it to third parties, do not allow humans to read it, and do not use it to develop, improve or train generalized or non-personalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

How we protect sensitive data

We protect sensitive data — access to your Google account (OAuth tokens), email app passwords, license and certificate data — with the following technical and organizational measures:

  • Encryption at rest on your computer. Google OAuth access and refresh tokens, app passwords for other email accounts and license activation data are stored encrypted with Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI), bound to your Windows user account. Other users of the computer cannot read them, and they cannot be copied to and used on another computer.
  • Encryption in transit. All communication uses encrypted connections only (TLS): HTTPS to the license server, HTTPS to Google APIs, and mandatory TLS to other mail servers. The application never sends data over an unencrypted connection.
  • Secure sign-in with Google. Sign-in uses the OAuth 2.0 flow for installed applications in your system browser with PKCE; the application never sees or stores your Google password.
  • Server authenticity. Every license server response is digitally signed and the application accepts it only if the signature is valid; the signing key is stored encrypted on the server and never leaves it.
  • No secrets in readable form. Activation codes and license access tokens are stored on the server only as cryptographic hashes (SHA-256). The application never sees or stores the smart card PIN, and the private signing key never leaves the card.
  • Restricted server access. The server is protected by a firewall (only the required ports are open); its administrative interface is reachable only locally on the server, and the administrator dashboard only over HTTPS with a password and two-factor authentication (TOTP), with lockout after repeated failed attempts. Only the server administrators can access the server files, database and backups. Every administrative action is permanently logged.
  • Data minimization. We request from Google only the permissions for your email address and for sending email, without reading mail. Documents, file names, case numbers, names of parties, email content, recipient addresses and Google tokens are never sent to our server. IP addresses are deleted after 12 months.
  • Security incidents. In the event of a personal data breach, we will notify the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection and the affected users in accordance with the Serbian Law on Personal Data Protection.
